## body

A personal attack replaces checking a reason with judging the speaker. A straw man changes someone’s position into an easier target. Finding a faulty argument does not automatically prove the opposite claim.

## example

“We need breaks between tasks.” — “So you do not want to learn at all!” The reply changes a proposal about breaks into a different claim.

## caution

Do not use the name of a fallacy as an insult. Restate the actual idea and ask a question.

## task

Write a four-line dialogue with one distorted position. Rewrite the reply to address the real proposal.

## criteria

The original idea is preserved; the reply addresses it; no personal labels are used.

## reflection

Can a true idea be supported by a poor argument?
