## body

Before objecting, try to state the other view so its supporter recognises it. Name a strong reason, then identify the disagreement. Fair presentation does not require agreement.

## example

One learner wants group tasks to practise cooperation. Another wants individual tasks to work at their own pace. Both goals deserve attention.

## caution

Do not claim the other person approved your summary. If they are absent, label it as your reconstruction.

## task

Write two reasons for group work and two for individual work. Suggest an option that considers both goals.

## criteria

Neither view is mocked; reasons are relevant; a shared option is not presented as compulsory.

## reflection

What can the sides agree on while keeping different views?
