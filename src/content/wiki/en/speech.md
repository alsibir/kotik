## body

Choose a narrow question and one main idea. Start with a familiar situation, explain a view, give reasons and finish with a conclusion. Rehearsal checks clarity rather than perfect memorisation.

## example

Topic: “Why have a quiet reading area?” Begin with a situation, give two reasons and a limitation, then suggest trying a solution.

## caution

One attempt does not measure your ability. Feedback should describe specific actions.

## task

Prepare a two-to-three-minute speech. Make an outline, rehearse and present to a mentor. Ask for two things that worked and one improvement.

## criteria

A main idea is clear; reasons support it; timing is appropriate; one next improvement is chosen.

## reflection

What should stay in the next speech, and what needs practice?
