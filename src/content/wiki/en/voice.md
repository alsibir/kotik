## body

A pause separates units of meaning. A steady pace gives listeners time to follow. Choose a volume that suits the room. Start with a comfortable posture and calm, natural breathing.

## example

Read “First, check the source. Then, draw a conclusion” with a pause between the actions. Compare it with reading without a pause.

## caution

Do not hold your breath, force your volume or continue with discomfort. Clarity matters more than a special voice.

## task

Choose four sentences. Mark meaningful pauses. Read twice and ask a mentor to retell the main point. Recording is optional.

## criteria

Words are audible; endings are clear; pauses support meaning; speaking feels comfortable.

## reflection

What helped the listener: pace, a pause or emphasis?
