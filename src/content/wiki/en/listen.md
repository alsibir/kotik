## body

Listening means letting someone finish, noticing the main point and checking your understanding. Retelling a view does not mean agreeing with it. A clarifying question asks for information that is missing.

## example

“Do you mean you need a quiet corner to finish your drawing?” checks understanding. “You just dislike company” guesses for someone else.

## caution

Do not assign hidden motives to another person. Ask them to check even a convincing retelling.

## task

Ask a mentor to talk about a favourite activity for one minute. Retell the main idea in two sentences. Ask one open question and revise your retelling.

## criteria

The speaker finished; the retelling was checked; the question did not suggest a preferred answer.

## reflection

What changed after your clarifying question?
