## body

A factual claim can be checked through observation or a reliable source. An opinion expresses a judgement. A guess suggests an explanation that still needs checking. Sounding plausible does not make a claim true.

## example

“The book has 120 pages” can be checked. “The book is boring” is an opinion. “The author wanted to surprise us” is a guess about intention.

## caution

One sentence may mix facts and opinions. “Everyone knows” is not evidence.

## task

Write six statements about an imaginary school. Label facts, opinions and guesses. Explain how each factual claim could be checked.

## criteria

Two examples of each kind; a way to check each factual claim; guesses are not presented as established events.

## reflection

Which statement was hardest to label, and why?
