## body

Describe what happened, name your feeling, explain a need and ask for a clear action. A feeling does not prove that someone is at fault. A request allows a refusal and discussion of another solution.

## example

“I can hear music while I read. I feel irritated because I need quiet. Could you turn it down for fifteen minutes?”

## caution

“I feel that you are selfish” judges a person. A communication pattern does not oblige someone to agree.

## task

Invent a situation about loud music or a shared game. Write an observation, a feeling, a need and a request. Add another possible solution.

## criteria

No insults or guessed motives; the request is clear and possible; a “no” is allowed.
