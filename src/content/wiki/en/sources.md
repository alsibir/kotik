## body

Check the author, date, original publication and evidence. Several websites may repeat one rumour: that is not independent confirmation. When evidence is missing, “not yet known” is an accurate conclusion.

## example

Two websites announce an exhibition, but both quote one old notice. To plan a visit, check the organiser’s current information.

## caution

A bright headline and a confident tone do not replace checking. Lack of confirmation does not always prove a claim false.

## task

With a mentor, choose a neutral report about a museum or science event. Find the original source, its date and another independent account if available.

## criteria

Links and dates are recorded; repeated reports are distinguished from independent sources; unknowns are marked.
