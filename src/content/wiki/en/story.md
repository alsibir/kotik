## body

A listener needs a character, a situation, a difficulty, an action and an outcome. Not every story needs a moral. One event and a few useful details are enough for a practice speech.

## example

A character builds a paper bridge. The first model falls. A different fold helps it hold a book. The change connects the beginning and the ending.

## caution

Do not present fiction as an event involving a real person. Extra details can hide the main point.

## task

Invent a story about a paper bridge or a lost bookmark. Make a five-point plan and tell it in one minute.

## criteria

The character and difficulty are clear; an action changes the situation; the story has an ending.

## reflection

Which detail helped the listener picture the event?
