## body

You may refuse a request that does not work for you. A short refusal needs no long excuse. Offering an alternative is optional. If threats or bullying begin, safety matters more than continuing negotiations.

## example

“No, I cannot share a password.” “I cannot play now. Could we arrange a time tomorrow?”

## caution

Respectful words do not guarantee a respectful response. The person making threats is responsible for them.

## task

With a mentor, act out three imaginary requests: share a password, change a game time, give away a personal object. Practise a refusal and a safe next step.

## criteria

The refusal is clear; private data stays private; asking an adult for help is an option under pressure.

## reflection

Which trusted adult could help? Keep the answer private.
