First name what is visible: objects, figures, light and colour. Separate observation from impression: “a dark sky” and “an anxious mood” are different descriptions.

Then ask a question: where is the character looking? Why is one detail lit up? An answer remains an interpretation unless the artist’s intention is known.

Write a three-sentence museum label: an observation, an impression and a question. When using a reproduction, credit the artist, title and image source.
