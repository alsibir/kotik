Imagine that one person already knows how to make presentations and the other is just learning. An equal number of slides looks like an equal share. Yet the time and difficulty may differ.

Fairness can be discussed in terms of equal shares, needs, contributions and agreed rules. These approaches sometimes lead to different decisions.

Discuss two ways to divide the task. Name one strength and one limitation of each. What further information would help?
