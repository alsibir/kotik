When reading, separate three questions: what did the character do, what might have influenced them, and how should the action be judged? The answers need not match.

Explaining a lie through fear does not make the lie right. Look for words and events in the text that support the explanation.

Choose a short scene. Record the action, two possible reasons and evidence from the text. If evidence is missing, label the idea as a guess.
